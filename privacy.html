2. What we collect and why

Newsletter and launch updates

  • What: your email address and a record of your consent (the text you agreed to, its version, and the date and time).
  • Why: to email you about the launch of Else, news and offers.
  • Legal basis (EU/EEA): your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • How long: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We then delete your email within 30 days, except for a minimal record of your consent and its withdrawal, kept only as long as needed to show that we respected your choice.

Emails you send us

  • What: your email address, name (if you include it) and the content of your message.
  • Why: to read and reply to your inquiry.
  • Legal basis (EU/EEA): our legitimate interest in answering messages sent to us (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • How long: as long as needed to handle your inquiry, and then up to 12 months.

Technical data when you visit the site

  • What: your IP address, browser type and the date and time of your visit.
  • Why: these are processed by our hosting and font providers to deliver the website and keep it secure. We do not use them to identify or profile you.
  • Legal basis (EU/EEA): our legitimate interest in running a working and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Visit statistics

  • What: when you open our home page, we record the date and time, the page, the website you came from (if any) whether you use a mobile or desktop screen, and your device's time zone (for example "America/New_York"), which tells us your approximate region. We do not record your IP address or anything that identifies you.
  • Why: to know how many people visit the site.
  • Legal basis (EU/EEA): our legitimate interest in measuring the audience of our website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • How long: up to 24 months.

3. Cookies and tracking

We do not use cookies, browser storage, advertising pixels or any technology that tracks you across websites or recognizes you between visits. Our visit statistics are anonymous counts (see above). That's why you don't see a cookie banner.

4. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted or cross-context behavioral advertising. We only use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Google (Google Workspace / Google Sheets): stores the list of newsletter signups and our anonymous visit statistics.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages): hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts: delivers the typeface used on this website.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. International transfers

These providers may process data in the United States. Where data from the EU/EEA is transferred, we rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified providers, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, and you can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in every email or by writing to us.

If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, you also have the right to:

  • restrict or object to how we process your data;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing done before;
  • file a complaint with a data protection authority, such as the Spanish Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es) or the one where you live.

We will reply to your request within one month.
